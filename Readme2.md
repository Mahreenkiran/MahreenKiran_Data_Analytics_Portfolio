## 1. AdventureWorks Sales and Customer Performance Analysis

**Tools:** SQL Server • T-SQL • Power BI • DAX • Power Query • Excel • GitHub

This project uses the **Microsoft AdventureWorks2025 sample database** to analyse company sales from a commercial and operational perspective.

The objective was to understand **what drives revenue, which customers and products generate the greatest value, how performance differs across territories and salespeople, and where operational improvement opportunities may exist**.

### Dataset

The project uses the **Microsoft AdventureWorks2025 sample database**, a relational dataset representing a fictional manufacturing and sales organisation.

The analysis focuses on commercial data covering:

- customers
- sales orders
- sales order details
- products
- product categories and subcategories
- sales territories
- salespeople
- order fulfilment information

The dataset supports analysis across **sales performance, customer behaviour, product demand, territory performance and operational efficiency**.

### Business Questions

The analysis investigated questions such as:

- How are revenue and orders changing over time?
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
- Which customers contribute the greatest commercial value?
- Does high order frequency always indicate high customer value?
- How does performance vary across territories?
- Which salespeople generate the strongest results?
- Which products have little or no recorded sales activity?
- How efficiently are customer orders fulfilled?

### Analysis Areas

- total revenue and order performance
- average order value
- annual and month-on-month sales growth
- product and category performance
- category revenue contribution
- top customers
- customer order frequency
- customer value segmentation
- inactive and never-purchased customers
- territory performance
- salesperson performance
- low-performing and zero-sales products
- order fulfilment analysis
- order value segmentation
- data-quality validation

### Selected Findings

- **Revenue increased by approximately 38.18% between the complete calendar years 2023 and 2024**, rising from approximately **$31.60M to $43.67M**. Growth was driven by substantially higher order and unit volumes despite a reduction in average order value.

- **Bikes generated approximately 86.17% of total company revenue**, making the category the dominant commercial revenue source. Road Bikes and Mountain Bikes were the strongest subcategories.

- **244 high-value customers generated approximately 65.10% of total company revenue**, showing substantial concentration within a relatively small customer segment.

- The **Top 10 customers contributed only 7.21% of total revenue**, indicating that revenue concentration was spread across the broader high-value customer group rather than being dependent on only a handful of accounts.

- **Southwest was the highest-revenue territory**, generating approximately **$24.18M**, while other territories displayed different combinations of order volume and average order value.

- **Linda Mitchell generated the highest salesperson revenue, while Jillian Carson processed more orders**, showing that order volume alone does not fully represent salesperson performance.

### SQL Techniques Demonstrated

`JOINs` • `Multi-Table JOINs` • `CTEs` • `Subqueries` • `CASE`  
`Window Functions` • `LAG` • `ROW_NUMBER` • `NTILE`  
`Aggregations` • `Percentage Contribution` • `Customer Segmentation`  
`Date Analysis` • `NULL Handling` • `Data-Quality Validation`

### What This Project Demonstrates

This project demonstrates practical **commercial and operational analytics**, including sales trend analysis, customer segmentation, product performance, territory comparison, salesperson evaluation and KPI development.

It also demonstrates the ability to distinguish between related but different business measures — for example, **revenue, order volume and average order value** — rather than assessing performance using a single metric.

## 2. Patient Conditions, Treatment and Healthcare Utilisation Analysis

**Tools:** SQL Server • T-SQL • Power BI • DAX • Power Query • Excel

This project analyses **synthetic Synthea healthcare data** to understand patient activity, healthcare utilisation, clinical patterns, treatment activity and healthcare costs.

The objective was not simply to count healthcare records. The analysis investigated **which patient groups use healthcare services most frequently, how clinical burden changes across the population, where healthcare activity is concentrated and what drives healthcare expenditure**.

### Dataset

The analysis includes:

- **1,163 patients**
- **61,459 healthcare visits**
- **38,094 condition records**
- **56,430 medication records**
- **83,823 procedure records**

### Analysis Areas

- patient demographics

- high-utilisation patient identification
- age and gender comparisons

- condition burden
- medication activity
- procedure activity
- healthcare cost analysis

The project also demonstrates that data-quality issues should be investigated rather than hidden.

Logical validation identified:

- **5 medication records** with invalid date sequences

These issues were documented and considered when interpreting longitudinal results.

## 3. E-Commerce Marketing, Conversion and Customer Journey Analysis

**Tools:** SQL Server • T-SQL • Power BI • DAX • Power Query • GitHub

This project analyses approximately three years of synthetic e-commerce activity to understand **how visitors discover a website, engage with content, progress through the customer journey and convert into paying customers**.

The project connects marketing acquisition with website behaviour, order performance, profitability, product performance and refunds.

### Dataset Scale

- **472,871** website sessions
- **1,188,124** pageviews
- **32,313** completed orders
- **40,025** order items
- **1,731** refund records
- **4** products

### Business Questions

The analysis investigated questions such as:

- Which marketing channels generate the strongest commercial performance?
- How does conversion differ between desktop and mobile?
- Which landing pages perform most effectively?
- Where do customers leave the purchase funnel?
- Are returning users more likely to purchase?
- How does session engagement relate to conversion?
- Do multi-item orders generate greater value?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- How significant are refunds?
- How are revenue and conversion changing over time?

### Selected Findings

- Desktop conversion was approximately **8.50%**, compared with **3.09% on mobile**.
- Paid Search generated approximately **$1.48M in net revenue**.
- The largest major funnel drop occurred between **Product Views and Cart**.
- Repeat-user sessions converted more successfully than non-repeat sessions.
- Converted sessions lasted substantially longer than non-converted sessions.
- Multi-item orders generated considerably higher average revenue and gross profit.
- **The Original Mr. Fuzzy** contributed approximately **62.5% of total item revenue**.
- Approximately **5.33% of orders** were associated with refunds.

`CTEs` • `ROW_NUMBER` • `LAG` • `RANK` • `DENSE_RANK`  
`PERCENTILE_CONT` • `Window Functions` • `Rolling Windows`  
`Conditional Aggregation` • `EXISTS` • `CASE` • `COALESCE` • `NULLIF`  
`Month-on-Month Analysis` • `Customer Journey Funnel Analysis`

### What This Project Demonstrates

This project demonstrates end-to-end **digital and commercial analytics**, combining advanced SQL, marketing performance analysis, customer behaviour, funnel analysis, profitability and interactive Power BI reporting.

# Analytical Approach

Each project follows a consistent analytical workflow.

### 1. Define the Business Question

Understand what decision or performance issue the analysis is intended to support.

### 2. Understand the Data

Review table grain, fields, primary keys, relationships and available measures.

### 3. Validate Data Quality

Identify missing values, duplicates, broken relationships, financial inconsistencies and logical anomalies.

### 4. Engineer Analytical Variables

Create fields required for segmentation, time analysis, customer behaviour, profitability and reporting.

### 5. Analyse with SQL

Use SQL to investigate trends, relationships, performance differences and business drivers.

### 6. Develop KPIs

Translate analytical results into measurable business indicators.

### 7. Build Power BI Reporting

Design interactive dashboards that allow performance to be explored visually.

### 8. Interpret the Results

Move beyond describing numbers to explain **why the result matters**.

### 9. Recommend Actions

Translate analytical findings into evidence-based recommendations.
